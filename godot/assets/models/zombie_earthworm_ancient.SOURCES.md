# zombie_earthworm_ancient

Geometry and PBR textures generated with Meshy from an inspected design reference. Original files and requests: `meshy_output/earthworm_quality/zombie_earthworm_ancient/`.

Design: `01a0cdef-0af9-7649-9baf-21cc49a2b2ed`; 9 credits.

Image to 3D: `01a0cdf0-a428-74e9-9eba-5a0092497b84`; 35 credits.

72,745 triangles. Source-resolution PBR maps retained with lossless WebP compression (4K colour/normal; source roughness resolution recorded in artifacts/earthworm-mesh-quality.json).

22-joint connected annelid skeleton; fixed bone lengths and rigid maw. Seven locally authored animation clips, including recovery. These animations are not Meshy API output.

Prepared by `tools/prepare_earthworms.mjs`. Neutral height 1.7 m; scaled to 14 m / 19 m in game.
