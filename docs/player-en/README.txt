RemZ - Windows x64

START
Extract the whole ZIP into one folder, then start RemZ.exe.
Keep RemZ.pck and all three DLLs next to the EXE. Godot does not need to be installed.
To pass the game on, zip the complete contents of this folder.

WHAT'S NEW IN THIS RELEASE
- A calmer arrival: follow one immediate objective, then learn the controls
  through Vendor's optional introduction and short, contextual lessons.
  Experienced players can skip the guidance.
- Z now follows the printed letter on both QWERTZ and QWERTY keyboards.
  Each class receives its own explanation; Assassin keeps its V teleport.
- A redesigned fieldbook with clear navigation, contextual actions, quests,
  discoveries, augments and checkpoints. Open it with K or from the pause menu.
- A compact weapon and ammo display at the bottom right leaves more room
  to see the pistol. Menus and the minimap respect the available screen space.
- Interaction prompts match the action performed. Confirming a menu with
  Enter no longer starts a wave as well. Menu, pause and checkpoint fixes
  cover both Forest and The Planes.

CAMPAIGN
Start game opens the region map. Forest and The Planes are playable;
the other four regions remain marked Under construction.
A standard expedition has 25 waves followed by a final defence or extraction.
The region is secured only after that finale succeeds. The Ten wave assault
challenge shortens the waves to ten and still includes the finale.
Choose a challenge or enter a shared run code in the fieldbook before wave one.
Personal bests and victories remain saved on the campaign map.
In co-op, the host selects the region and the entire team receives completion.
After victory, Map selection returns to the map. Escape leaves the selection.

LANGUAGE
The game starts in English. For German: Settings > Language > Deutsch.
The choice is saved and switches the game immediately.

CONTROLS
WASD: move, Mouse: look around, Left click/Right click: shoot/aim.
Shift: sprint, Ctrl: crouch, R: reload, 1-9/0: quick bar, Mouse wheel: weapon.
G: grenade, H: melee strike, E: the nearby interaction shown on screen.
F: flashlight, or repair a nearby tower.
I: inventory, M: expand/restore the map, Q: quest tracker, Hold Tab: leaderboard.
K: fieldbook, also available in the pause menu. Escape closes it.
Z: class action using the printed Z key, including on QWERTZ; Shift+Z works too.
Assassin: V for the chosen teleport after unlocking it at class level 15.
T: tower planner. Pick a tower and left click a clear spot to build.
R/Mouse wheel: turn the preview or the tower under the cursor; Shift+R turns back.
Drag a ground tower to move it. +/- zooms. T/Escape closes the planner.
The forest hut has six roof slots; click a free roof ring or slot button to build.
Normal expeditions allow 40 towers per team, including roof towers.
The Four tower challenge reduces that shared limit to four.
E at a ground tower: mount/dismount. Left click: fire. Hold right click: zoom.
R at an unoccupied nearby ground tower: enter manual alignment, then turn with
R/Mouse wheel and confirm with E. Upgrades/dismantling are at Mechanic.
B in Forest: drop Rem Dollars. B on The Planes: open purchased fortification kits;
choose a kit, rotate with R/Mouse wheel and place with E. B/Escape cancels.
E at the drone station upstairs in the forest hut: choose a drone.
WASD/Mouse: fly and aim, Space/Ctrl: climb/descend, Left click: fire,
R/Escape: recall. Your body remains at the station.
Enter during gameplay: start the next wave early. It does not bypass menu input.
Escape: pause, F11: fullscreen. Adjust graphics, sound and mouse sensitivity
in the start/pause menu.

EXPEDITIONS
Both maps include class actions, augments, optional operations, cooperative
supplies, discoveries, performance records and quiet-intermission checkpoints.
The Planes adds outposts, supply routes, field fortifications, shooting-range
challenges, cornfield signals and weather-driven fires. Forest includes the
Secret Night, field trials and a final radio defence.
The fieldbook pauses solo play; co-op keeps running while you read.
Only the host saves or continues a cooperative checkpoint. Rejoin with the
same teammates and distinct player names before continuing.

MULTIPLAYER
Main menu > Multiplayer. Up to four players.
Online lobby: the host creates a lobby and shares the six-letter join code;
teammates join with the code over the internet, without Hamachi, port
forwarding or an Epic account. Direct / LAN / Hamachi uses an IP address
and UDP port 24567. All players need the same current build and its DLLs.
Details: MULTIPLAYER.md. Each player can choose their own language;
host messages appear in that player's language.

MORE INFORMATION
VERSION.txt and BUILD-INFO.json: build, checks and checksums.
README.txt (this file), MULTIPLAYER.md, DEFENSE.md and PROGRESSION.md: player
guides. The German versions are included too: LIESMICH.txt, MEHRSPIELER.md,
VERTEIDIGUNG.md and FORTSCHRITT.md.
GODOT-LICENSES.txt, VALVE-LICENSE.txt and the ASSETS files: license and source notes.
Diagnostic logs are written to the logs folder while you play.
